# VoiceRambler Free (Samsung / Android)

A 100% free, zero-subscription AI voice dictation app powered by the **Google AI Studio Free Tier** (`gemini-3.5-transcribe-live`, `gemini-2.5-flash`, `gemini-3.5-flash-lite`, `gemini-3.8-flash`).

## Features
- **Auto 4-Language Detection:** Seamlessly handles **English, Hindi, Hinglish (Roman script), and Odia**—even mixed mid-sentence.
- **Multi-Specialty Medical Terminology:** Built-in clinical vocabulary biasing and strict safety rules that preserve drug doses, decimals, units, laterality, and negations (`no`, `absent`, `negative for`).
- **Intelligent Punctuation & Formatting:** Infers punctuation, paragraphs, and bullet/numbered lists automatically.
- **Transparent, Movable Floating Mic:** Auto-shows when a text field is focused, drags anywhere on screen, vibrates subtly on tap, and supports **long-press to undo** the last dictation.
- **Cursor-Aware Insertion:** Inserts text at your current cursor/selection without overwriting surrounding paragraphs.
- **Privacy:** `allowBackup="false"` keeps your API key out of cloud backups. *(Avoid dictating identifiable patient names or IDs on the free tier.)*
